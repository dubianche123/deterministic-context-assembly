# PAM∃LA research deck images

These 256 images are a selection from the PAM∃LA dataset
(<https://huggingface.co/datasets/bethgelab/PAMELA>, revision `31f79af`),
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They were resized to 480 px WebP; nothing else was changed. The repository's
MIT license covers its code, not these images. The research build credits the
dataset on its entry screen.
