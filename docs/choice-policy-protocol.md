# Conservative choice policy v1 — fixed before its outer evaluation

2026-10-21. This changes the predictor, not the fixtures, exposure, labels, prefixes, test rounds, metrics or seeds in `fast-thinker-diagnostic-protocol.md`. The old 12-feature model remains an independent reference. The outer holdout was already inspected during diagnosis; reuse is development regression, not independent validation. No parameter search or selection using that holdout is permitted.

## One fixed candidate, one training-only gate

Keep the same 12 card features and STOP. Let `n` be the number of completed training rounds with a nonempty eligible display. Candidate Gaussian card-weight precision is **`1.5 * (1 + 12 / max(1, n))`**; STOP precision remains **0.5**. This fixed dimension/history rule strengthens the original prior for small samples and tends to its original precision with more history. No feature or timing is added. Its constants are design choices, not a claimed optimal setting.

Before completed round t, fit this candidate on completed rounds strictly before t. Freeze it throughout round t; for each finally-kept card c, among remaining cards A, add **`log(len(A)) + u(c) - logsumexp(u(A))`** to cumulative log evidence. STOP is excluded from content evidence. A forced last card has zero gain. Only after scoring the round is the candidate refitted with it. Open rounds never contribute to the gate or the next completed-round candidate. Use stable logits directly: no probability floor, log-evidence clipping, reset, best window, or same-round refit.

The final current-prefix gate opens only if **`logE >= log(20)`**. It is not latched: later counterevidence can close it. With no sufficient content evidence, effective card weights are **exactly zero** and STOP is fitted on the supplied training prefix with the existing stop-only estimator. With evidence, use the full-prefix candidate. Raw candidate posterior and effective scoring parameters are separately labeled. Fallback has no personalized recommendations, learned motif ordering or arbitrary ID-based top cards.

A final open round may update provisional candidate parameters and STOP from its observed picks. It does not advance the evidence or completed-round count, and is excluded from `path` until closed. Candidate uncertainty is explicitly labeled; a fallback effective zero point is not presented as a posterior centered at zero, and uniform type placeholders are not displayed as estimated types. The production validation keeps the engine's existing forced-STOP denominator; the diagnostic full-sequence metric continues to exclude those zero-information steps. They are labeled separately.

Under the narrow null that each retained-card label is conditionally uniform over remaining cards given continuing, each predictive likelihood-ratio factor has conditional mean one. The resulting nonnegative e-process has an ideal-null anytime threshold-crossing bound of 1/20. This is **not 95% confidence in a preference**, and does not cover human position effects, nonuniform click order or outcome filtering that violate that null. A failure to pass means insufficient evidence for this policy, not proof of no preference. The gate cannot anticipate a future reversal unseen in its prefix.
